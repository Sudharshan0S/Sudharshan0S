### 1. 🧠 CrowdVision AI — Stampede Risk Estimation

### 2. 🧬 NeuroScan AI — Brain CT Classification
**Deep Learning · Medical AI research prototype**

A research prototype that classifies brain CT slices into **Normal, Ischemic, or Hemorrhagic** categories and presents class probabilities with Grad-CAM heatmaps.

- 🧠 ResNet50V2-based image classification
- 🔍 Grad-CAM heatmaps for visual interpretation
- 🔌 React frontend with FastAPI inference backend
- 📈 Reported held-out test performance: **90.3% accuracy** and **0.847 macro-F1** on 934 CT slices

**Stack:** `Python` `TensorFlow` `ResNet50V2` `FastAPI` `React` `OpenCV`

> ⚠️ Research prototype for learning and experimentation, not a clinical diagnostic tool.

[🔗 View Repository](https://github.com/Sudharshan0S/Brain_Stroke_Detection)
